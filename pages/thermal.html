Thermal States on Mittag-Leffler Fock Space

Natanael Alpay and Tiju Cherian John

Published research

Research Summary

We study number states and thermal states on Mittag-Leffler Fock space over the slitted plane. Generalized creation and annihilation operators provide the framework for extending constructions familiar from the classical Fock-space setting.

The project connects reproducing kernel methods with operator-theoretic questions in quantum theory.
