Commutative Doubly-Idempotent Semirings

Natanael Alpay and Peter Jipsen

Published research

Research Summary

A commutative doubly-idempotent semiring has idempotent addition and multiplication. Its additive join operation has identity 0, so x ∨ 0 = x; multiplication has identity 1, distributes over joins, and satisfies x · 0 = 0.

We describe these structures when the multiplicative semilattice is a chain, showing that their isomorphism classes are counted by Catalan numbers. We also connect Boolean examples with singleton-rooted preorder forests, leading to algorithms for constructing finite semirings.
