Mathematical Structures in 3D and Virtual Reality

Natanael Alpay and Peter Jipsen

Finite algebraic structures give rise to lattices and other partially ordered sets that can be difficult to understand from a two-dimensional drawing. This project uses interactive three-dimensional models to explore their organization.

We developed JavaScript visualizations using Babylon.js for exploration in a browser, with virtual-reality support in the original project. Examples include hypercubes and lattices of preorders.
