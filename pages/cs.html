I work on machine learning, statistical modeling, and scientific computing, with an emphasis on the mathematics behind the methods and their implementation. My projects range from pharmacovigilance and financial risk to kernel methods and mathematical visualization.

Research and Machine Learning

Bellman Deep Hedging and Portfolio Compression

Work in progress

I am studying how low-dimensional representations of financial portfolios affect hedging decisions. The project connects mathematical bounds on representation and learning errors with Bellman-based benchmarks and the development of neural-network trading strategies, accounting for transaction costs and trading constraints.

Portfolio compression · Risk-sensitive optimization · Dynamic programming

FAERS: Machine Learning for Drug Safety Signal Prioritization

I developed and evaluated gradient-boosted models to prioritize drug–adverse-event reporting signals in the FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS). The study combines classical disproportionality measures with report-quality information and examines performance on future quarters, calibration, and the contribution of different feature groups.

Gradient boosting · Temporal validation · Calibration

VAERS: Early Prioritization of Vaccine–Symptom Signals

Using the Vaccine Adverse Event Reporting System (VAERS), I built a workflow that ranks vaccine–symptom pairs by their likelihood of meeting a reporting-signal criterion in the following quarter. The project compares logistic regression and calibrated gradient boosting with classical statistical scores to prioritize pairs for expert review.

Logistic regression · Gradient boosting · Forecasting

Poster abstract CHOC conference presentation

Complex Stochastic Gradient Descent and Kernel Regression

In joint work with Emeric Battaglia, I study stochastic gradient descent for complex-valued kernel regression. The project combines convergence and directional-bias analysis with numerical experiments in complex reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces, including the recovery of superoscillatory functions and Blaschke products.

Stochastic optimization · Kernel methods · Numerical analysis

Preprint on arXiv
