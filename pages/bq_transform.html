The Mittag-Leffler Bargmann Transform

Natanael Alpay and Kamal Diki

Published research

Research Summary

We construct and investigate a Mittag-Leffler version of the Segal–Bargmann transform. The work relates the transform to Fourier analysis and studies creation and annihilation operators involving fractional differentiation.

We also develop a quaternionic extension in the slice-hyperholomorphic setting, connecting complex and hypercomplex function-space methods.
